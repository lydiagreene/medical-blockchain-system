Built for zero tolerance of fraud
Four layers of protection — all working together
Every credential goes through a stack of independent security checks before you ever see a result.
Layer 1 — Immutability
Ethereum Blockchain
Every credential is recorded as an on-chain transaction. Altering a single record would require re-mining the entire chain — mathematically impossible. The hash is public and auditable by anyone.
Layer 2 — Decentralisation
IPFS Decentralised Storage
Credential documents and practitioner photos are stored on IPFS — a distributed network with no single point of failure. Content is addressed by its cryptographic hash, not a fragile URL. Deletion is impossible once pinned.
Layer 3 — Identity
Biometric Face Verification
A live webcam photo is compared against the registered practitioner photo stored on IPFS. AI face-matching confirms the person presenting the credential is the same person who was registered — preventing credential lending or impersonation.
Layer 4 — Intelligence
AI Fraud Detection
Every verification attempt is scored by a machine learning model trained on access patterns — time of day, request frequency, IP clusters, face-match confidence. Suspicious patterns are flagged and surfaced in the admin fraud dashboard in real time.
